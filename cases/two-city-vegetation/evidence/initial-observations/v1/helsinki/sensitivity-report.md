# Helsinki: mapped vegetation experiment

6 of 34 zones with valid non-water pixels are below the declared 40% vegetation land-share threshold. 144,068 published 2025 residents are attached to those zones; this is not population without green access or exposed to heat. Land cover represents 2021.

- WorldCover is a modeled annual 2021 land-cover classification, not a current tree inventory, canopy percentage, NDVI, park-access measure, cooling effect or air temperature.
- Vegetation includes cropland, grassland, wetlands, shrubland, tree cover and moss/lichen. Tree-class area is not fractional tree canopy.
- Native geographic pixels have latitude-dependent physical size; the nearest-neighbour 10 m local metric grid is an analysis grid, not new source resolution. Boundary pixel-centre area approximates polygon area.
- Land-share denominator excludes class 80 and NoData 0. All-surface share, water and NoData are reported separately. Unknown class codes fail validation.
- The 20% low-vegetation threshold is an exploratory scenario, not a policy standard. Sensitivity changes use the same historical raster, not vegetation change over time.
- 2025 aggregate population and acquisition-date boundaries are temporally different from 2021 land cover. Residents are not located within pixels; population in low-share zones is not population without access or exposed to heat.
- Global published classification accuracy is not local validation or per-pixel confidence. The separate InputQuality product is not acquired through this public Map recipe; NoData coverage is not a confidence score.
- Initial-team tests in two cities do not meet the independent third-city final-product qualification rule.
